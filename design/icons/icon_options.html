FullResShare: icon options (A chosen and enlarged)

Each is shown as a rounded square and a circle (launchers crop icons differently), at home-screen size and small, and on a dark wallpaper.

A. Send the original

A photo with an arrow leaving it. Closest to the current icon; says "share a picture" at a glance.

B. Full frame

Viewfinder corners around a landscape: the whole picture, nothing trimmed or compressed. The most distinctive on a crowded home screen.

C. Photo as a file

A document with a photo inside it. States the app's actual trick most literally: the picture travels as a file.
